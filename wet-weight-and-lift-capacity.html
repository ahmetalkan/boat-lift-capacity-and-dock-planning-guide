Calculating Wet Boat Weight and Lift Capacity Margin

Boat lift ratings represent total suspended weight—including any wet weight left in the hull—not dry factory shipping weight.

Weight itemHow to estimate
Dry boat hullManufacturer published spec (verify whether engines are excluded)
Outboard / sterndrive enginesAdd full motor weight if dry hull spec omits propulsion
Fuel (full tank)Roughly 6.2 lb per US gallon for gasoline (~7.1 lb/gal for diesel)
Fresh water / holding / ballastRoughly 8.3 lb per US gallon (always drain ballast before lifting)
Batteries, bimini frame, anchor, gearTypically 300 to 1,000+ lb depending on vessel size and electronics
Safety & upgrade marginAdd roughly 20% headroom above loaded wet weight for wave bounce, rainwater, and longer cable life
